Our Story

A Legacy of Craftsmanship

"Every classic car tells a story. Our job is to make sure that story continues for generations to come."

Founded in 1985 by master mechanic James Heritage, our family-owned shop has grown from a small two-bay garage into a premier restoration facility spanning 25,000 square feet. We've had the privilege of restoring everything from barn-find survivors to concours-winning show cars, each project receiving the same meticulous attention to detail.

Our team of certified technicians, upholsterers, painters, and metalworkers combine traditional techniques with modern technology to achieve results that exceed factory specifications. We're not just fixing cars—we're preserving automotive history.
